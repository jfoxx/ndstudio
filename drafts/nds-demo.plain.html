What is the NDS framework?

A set of blocks and design tokens for building government sites with a consistent look and feel, based on sites like safedc.gov and americabydesign.gov.

How do I use the gov banner?

Add a Gov Banner block in the first section and fill in the two optional lines of text (e.g. official website line and initiative line).

Where can I see the design tokens?

Open styles/styles.css and look for the :root variables prefixed with --nds-.
